FAQs

◆ ◆ ◆

Do I need to RSVP now?

Not yet! Formal invitations will be sent by email in the coming months, and you'll have the opportunity to RSVP at that time. In the meantime, please fill out our Stay in Touch form so we have your details — and if you already know you won't be able to make it, you're welcome to let us know there too.

Is there a dress code?

We encourage guests to come dressed in formal wear. This could be a suit or a dress shirt with a tie, midi or maxi dresses. The ceremony and reception will both be held indoors.

After dinner, we want everyone on the dance floor! Feel free to bring a change of shoes so you can dance the night away in comfort. We'll have a designated spot to store dress shoes during the reception.

Can I bring a plus one?

Please check your invitation to see if a plus one has been included.

Can I bring my child?

Children are welcome if they're listed on your invitation. If you have any questions about your invite, don't hesitate to reach out.

Will meals accommodate dietary restrictions?

We want everyone to enjoy the meal! When formal invitations go out, you'll have the opportunity to share any dietary restrictions, and we'll work with The Fort Garry to make sure you're taken care of.

Will alcohol be served?

Drinks will be available at an open bar for all our guests.

Who should I contact if I have questions?
